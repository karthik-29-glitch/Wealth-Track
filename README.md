WealthTrack 📈

Stock & Mutual Fund Investment Portfolio Tracker

WealthTrack is a personal investment portfolio tracking application designed to help users organize, monitor, and analyze their stocks and mutual fund investments in one place.

The application provides a simple and user-friendly dashboard to record investments, monitor portfolio performance, and understand the overall value of an investment portfolio.

🔗 Live Demo: https://stock-mutual-fund-investment-portfolio.ai.studio

---

🚀 Features

- 📊 Investment Dashboard – View your overall investment portfolio in one place.
- 📈 Stock Tracking – Record and monitor individual stock investments.
- 💰 Mutual Fund Tracking – Maintain mutual fund investment details.
- 🧾 Transaction Records – Store investment and transaction information.
- 📉 Profit & Loss Tracking – Understand investment performance.
- 💵 Portfolio Value – Track the overall value of your investments.
- 📊 Portfolio Analysis – Analyze investments using organized data and visualizations.
- 🔍 Easy Data Management – Add, update, and manage investment records.
- 📱 User-Friendly Interface – Designed for simple and convenient portfolio management.

---

🎯 Purpose of the Project

Managing investments across different stocks and mutual funds can become difficult when information is stored in different places.

WealthTrack was developed to provide a centralized platform where users can maintain their investment information and get a clear overview of their portfolio.

The main goal is to make investment tracking simple, organized, and easy to understand.

---

🖥️ Application Overview

The application can be used to maintain information such as:

Investment Type| Information
Stocks| Company, quantity, purchase price, current value
Mutual Funds| Fund name, investment amount, units, NAV
Portfolio| Total investment, current value, returns
Transactions| Buy/Sell details and investment history
